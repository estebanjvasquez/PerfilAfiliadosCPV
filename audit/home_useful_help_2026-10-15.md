## 2. Decision: removed, with a useful replacement

**Replacement kept, because the actions it offers are not readily visible elsewhere:**
- the taxonomy map has **no sidebar item**: it is reached only from the manual or from a company's «Categorías CPV» tab;
- on mobile, the sidebar (and with it «Manual de usuario») is hidden behind ☰.

**What changed:**
- `FilamentInfoWidget` is removed from the panel's widget registration. Nothing is hidden with CSS, no vendor file is touched, and no other widget is removed.
- **New** `app/Filament/Widgets/ProfileHelpWidget.php` plus `resources/views/filament/widgets/profile-help-widget.blade.php`: the card «Ayuda para completar su perfil».
  - **Text:** «Antes de actualizar el perfil de su empresa, consulte la guía y explore las categorías de la Taxonomía CPV. El mapa es solo para ubicarse: las categorías se agregan desde la pestaña Categorías CPV de su empresa.» It does not imply that the map assigns categories.
  - **Buttons:** «Manual de usuario» → `UserManual::getUrl()`, and «Mapa de la taxonomía» → `TaxonomyMapPage::getUrl()`.
  - **Access:** the URLs are generated by the pages, not hard-coded. The card is visible only if `UserManual::canAccess()`, and the map button only if `TaxonomyMapPage::canAccess()`.
  - **Placement:** the same slot as the card it replaces (`$sort = -2`, right after the account card), not lazy, like the original.
  - **No** queries, metrics, company selector, notifications or remote services.
  - **Styling:** only Filament components (`x-filament::section`, `x-filament::button` as links) and the account card's own text classes. Spacing is inline, because the panel compiles no Tailwind theme of its own.
- **Preserved:** the account card and logout, the company widgets, role visibility, company authorization. There is no schema, mail, secret, taxonomy or company-data change.
